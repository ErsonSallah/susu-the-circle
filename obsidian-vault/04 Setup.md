# Setup · Build your circle

[[00 START HERE|Start]] · [[01 Home|Home]] · [[02 Rotation|Rotation]] · [[03 History|History]]

> [!success] Flexible by design
> In the **Setup** tab of `SUSU.html`, choose **USD, EUR, GHS, CFA XOF or CFA XAF**, contribution amount, weekly / biweekly / monthly intervals, and the first payout date. Rename and reorder members with mobile-friendly controls.

### Rules for editing

- **Rename a member:** Keeps the same cycle, contribution confirmations and positions.
- **Reorder members:** Starts a new cycle; records from the old cycle are archived.
- **Add or remove members:** Starts a new cycle with the new pot size, and archives any previous records. Minimum **2**, maximum **30**.
- **Change amount, currency, frequency, or first date:** Starts a new cycle. Historical activity keeps the original currency and amounts.
- **Start next cycle:** After every person has collected, the same order repeats, with the next payout date after the previous final one.

> [!tip] One banker is the source of truth.
> V1 is **single-device/single-banker**, not a networked multi-person app. Make a JSON backup before switching to a different phone or browser. This prevents conflicting copies.

**Launch the working app:** `SUSU.html` (open externally in your browser).
