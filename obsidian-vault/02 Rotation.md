# Rotation · Who collects next?

[[00 START HERE|Start]] · [[01 Home|Home]] · [[03 History|History]] · [[04 Setup|Setup]]

> [!info] The collection order
> The **Rotation** tab in `SUSU.html` displays every recipient, their scheduled payout date, the full pot, the number of confirmed contributions, and whether that round is **Current**, **Upcoming**, or **Paid out**.

| Round | Initial recipient | Initial schedule |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Person A | First payout date |
| 2 | Person B | + 1 interval |
| 3 | Person C | + 2 intervals |
| 4 | Person D | + 3 intervals |
| 5 | Person E | + 4 intervals |

Intervals are **7 days / 14 days / one calendar month**. Monthly payouts use the original day of the month where possible and clamp to the last day of shorter months.

> [!warning] Dates are a plan, not a payment trigger.
> The app **never automatically advances** based on time alone. The banker must record the completed payout. If a scheduled day passes, the round remains open.

**Launch the working app:** `SUSU.html` (open externally in your browser).
