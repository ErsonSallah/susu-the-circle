# History · The ledger

> [!info] Everything recorded in one place
> `SUSU.html` logs a dated event for every **contribution confirmed**, **contribution reversed**, and **payout completed**. The tab includes all-cycle/current/archive filters and a list of archived cycles.

### Backup & export

- **JSON backup:** Complete app state, current cycle, previous cycles and ledger. **Only JSON can restore** the working tracker.
- **CSV ledger:** Open or analyze the event history in spreadsheet apps.
- **Markdown snapshot:** Download a read-only summary to store or copy into Obsidian. It is not a live sync and cannot be imported to restore the app.

> [!warning] Keep your ledger safe
> The app cannot silently write into the Obsidian vault from an ordinary web browser. Local browser data is device/browser-specific and may be cleared. Export **JSON** after confirming contributions and payouts, and store your backups separately.

**Launch the working app:** `SUSU.html` (open externally in your browser).
