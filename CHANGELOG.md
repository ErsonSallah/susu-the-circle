# Changelog

## V1.0.0 — 2026-10-23

- Mobile-first SUSU dashboard (Home, Rotation, History, Setup).
- Contribution confirmation and payout tracking.
- Weekly, biweekly, and monthly rotation scheduling.
- USD, EUR, GHS, XOF, and XAF support.
- Local persistence, editable members, archived cycles, reversals.
- JSON backups and restores; CSV and Markdown exports.
- Green light and dark themes.
